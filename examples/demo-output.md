## Routing decisions

Each prompt is explained under every profile with `POST /v1/routing/explain`, which routes without calling a provider. Lambda is the success probability that a model's 95% lower bound (LCB) must reach under the profile. The cheapest model that reaches it is chosen, and when none does the policy's strongest model is. p is the calibrated success probability of the chosen model. Calibration is the block of the calibration set that the prompt's score falls in: k of its n requests succeeded. Expected cost is the policy's estimate for the prompt, from the typical output length of the model.
